How we approach leak detection hervey bay

We assess the available information and select suitable testing methods. Where appropriate, this can include acoustic listening, pressure testing, tracer gas, thermal imaging and pipe tracing. No single technique is suitable for every property or pipe system.

For an investigation, it helps to know where the water meter is, whether the leak is continuous, and whether any sections of the system can be isolated.

Our approach

More than one way to investigate a leak.

Different leaks call for different methods. Depending on the system and site, an investigation may use acoustic listening, isolation and pressure testing, tracer gas, thermal imaging or underground pipe tracing.

Our aim is to narrow down the likely leak location and help reduce unnecessary exploratory excavation. Results depend on site access, pipe materials, ground conditions and other limitations.

Signs you may have a hidden leak

01. Water meter movement when fixtures are off
02. Unexplained increase in water usage
03. Damp areas or persistent wet patches
04. Suspected underground pipe failure
