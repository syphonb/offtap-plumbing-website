The right method for your pipe.

We don't rely on a single tool. Each investigation combines methods chosen for your pipework and site, explained in plain English as we go.

01 · Often the first step

Acoustic leak
detection

Specialist listening equipment picks up the sound of water escaping pressurised pipework.

  • Water lines under lawns, paths or driveways
  • Concealed pipework in walls or under floors
  • Narrowing down the search area
02

Nitrogen testing

Confirms whether a section holds pressure, and can make a quiet leak easier to hear.

03

Tracer gas

For small leaks too quiet to hear, under concrete, pavers or long runs of lawn.

04

Thermal imaging

Surface temperature differences that point to moisture or a hot water line leaking in a slab or wall.

05

Pipe & service locating

Traces where the service actually runs, so repairs are planned with less guesswork.

No single method suits every leak. We'll tell you what we found, how confident we are, and what we recommend next.
