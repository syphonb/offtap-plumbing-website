Five methods. One clear answer.

01

Acoustic detection

Listening for water escaping pressurised pipe under lawns, paths and slabs.

02

Nitrogen testing

Confirming whether a section holds pressure, and making quiet leaks louder.

03

Tracer gas

Finding small leaks too quiet to hear, under concrete or pavers.

04

Thermal imaging

Warm spots from hot water lines leaking in slabs and walls.

05

Pipe locating

Tracing where the service actually runs, before anyone digs.

No single method suits every leak. We'll tell you what we found, how confident we are, and what we recommend next.
