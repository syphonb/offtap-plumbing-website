OffTap homepage concepts

Four separate directions for a leak-detection-first homepage. Add ?clean to any URL to hide placeholder content.

CONCEPT A

Under the Surface

Dark, calm and technical. An interactive house cutaway in the hero matches symptoms to methods. Lion used as a badge.

 CONCEPT B

Listen Closely

Built around acoustic detection. Lion as the hero character, live waveform, and a drag-the-sensor demo with optional sound.

 CONCEPT C

Field Notes

Light blueprint and job-sheet style. A scroll-driven site plan walks through each method to the marked leak. Lion as a stamp.

 CONCEPT D

Two Elements

Bright, friendly, built on the logo's blue/orange split. Lion hero between water and gas, a 3-question test finder, flip cards.
