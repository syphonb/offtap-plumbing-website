Five ways
to find it.

01

Acoustic detection

Specialist listening equipment picks up the sound of water escaping pressurised pipework under lawns, paths and slabs.

02

Nitrogen pressure testing

A controlled nitrogen test checks whether a section holds pressure and can make a quiet leak easier to hear.

03

Tracer gas

A specialist tracer gas is introduced into suitable pipework and detected at the surface to narrow down small, quiet leaks.

04

Thermal imaging

A thermal camera shows surface temperature differences that can point to moisture or a hot water line leaking in a slab.

05

Pipe & service locating

Electronic locating traces suitable underground services, so repairs can be planned with less guesswork.

No single method suits every leak. We choose a combination for your pipework and site, and tell you how confident we are.
